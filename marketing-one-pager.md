# CABLE – Make Dumb Appliances Smart

## What is CABLE?

CABLE is a small, universal connector that sits between **appliances** and **software**, translating wires and signals into modern APIs and automation. It upgrades existing machines into smart, connected, autonomous devices.

## Problem

- Most appliances are closed, offline, and isolated.
- Every manufacturer reinvents their own ecosystem.
- Users cannot easily integrate devices into their own workflows.

## Solution

CABLE provides:

- A **hardware module** that connects to appliance control lines.
- **Firmware** that understands signals and exposes capabilities.
- A **gateway server** that offers APIs and integrations.

Result: your fridge, printer, or washing machine becomes a participant in your digital world.

## Key Benefits

- Retrofit existing devices instead of replacing them.
- Unified way to monitor and control different brands and models.
- Local autonomy with optional cloud.
- Open, extensible, developer-friendly.

## Example Scenarios

- Printer orders its own ink and reports detailed status.
- Fridge alerts you before food spoils and syncs with your shopping list.
- Washing machine runs when energy is cheapest, then notifies your phone.

## Who Is It For?

- Makers and hardware hackers
- Smart home enthusiasts
- Appliance manufacturers
- System integrators

## Status

CABLE is in **early prototype** stage with open documentation, firmware skeleton, and a gateway server scaffold.

> CABLE is the bridge between the devices you already own and the intelligence they deserve.
